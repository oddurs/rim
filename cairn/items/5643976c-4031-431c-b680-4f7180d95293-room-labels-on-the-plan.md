## Why

A plan names its rooms. A label shows the result of the role rules, and lets the player see at a glance which rooms count as indoors.

## What

- Each enclosed room gets an anchored label at the free cell nearest its middle: the role, set in tracked capitals, and a line under it with its size and whether it's heated, or "open to the sky".
- A ring broken by one gap is labelled "not a room".
- Labels fade out when zoomed out, and roofs take over.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Labels use the anchored layer's collision nudging (§11)
- [x] The UI stays under budget with 40 rooms on screen

## 2026-09-26

Render bench close view (zoom 28, 8-cell rooms, about 39 on screen): ui 0.55-0.74 ms against the 1 ms budget. Labels sit on the anchored layer at priority -2, so pawn names win when they crowd. The label goes on the free cell nearest the middle of what's on screen of the room, so it stays in view as the camera moves; slivers under 4 cells at the screen edge get none.
