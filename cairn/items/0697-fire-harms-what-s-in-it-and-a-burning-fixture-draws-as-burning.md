## Why

Fire (0200) spreads and is put out, but its note lists three gaps. Items lying in a fire don't burn. Pawns in fire take no damage. A burning fixture has no draw of its own, so the flames under it show but the tree doesn't burn.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Items on a burning cell lose hp by flammability and are destroyed at none (test)
- [ ] A pawn in a burning cell takes burn damage (test)
- [ ] A burning tree or building draws as burning
