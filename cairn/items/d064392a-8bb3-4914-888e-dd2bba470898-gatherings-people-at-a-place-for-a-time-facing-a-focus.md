## Why

Funerals need people to come together; shared meals and parties are the next
users (DESIGN.md §4g).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A gathering has a place, a focus, invitees, a start and a duration
- [ ] Invitees walk there, stand or sit around the focus, and leave at the end
- [ ] Emergencies (drafted, starving, a raid) pull people out
- [ ] Events for started, joined, left and ended, with who attended
