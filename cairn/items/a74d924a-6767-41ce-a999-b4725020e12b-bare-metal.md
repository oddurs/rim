rim fast, stable and small: an all-hands with every session on it, from 2026-09-28, and no feature work until it's done. The plan, with its numbers and reasoning, is the user's doc: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/78a7670e-3413-40ea-9a5e-f6d59a4bf0f5

## Goal

Every budget holds on the Mac and on a 2017-class ultrabook (Intel UHD 620), and CI gates each one. Working targets, each of them the user's to change:
- whole frame (CPU + GPU + present) at 1080p: 8.3 ms, capped at 16.6 ms p99;
- world CPU: 2 ms;
- lighting GPU at the default preset: 1 ms;
- draw calls: 60 on the whole map;
- no frame over 2× the median;
- sim tick with weather on: mean 0.1 ms, p99 1 ms, max 4 ms;
- flaky tests: 0.
Features resume after three green nightlies in a row.

## Rules

- Feature freeze: a PR adds speed, fixes a flake, or deletes code.
- Before-and-after numbers in every PR: the same bench, and the same runner class on CI (check that the moving light pass and ui fall in the same band), or the Mac with the window visible and one bench at a time.
- One owner per hot file: light.rs is quiet-meadow's, draw.rs and mesh.rs silver-field's, autotest.rs green-forest's, main.rs calm-forest's.
