The map should answer a mouse and a trackpad the way each platform taught its users, and a right-click should never destroy anything.

## Goal

A trackpad pans with two fingers and zooms with a pinch; a mouse zooms with the wheel and pans with a middle or right drag. A right-click does the obvious safe thing or opens a menu, and nothing damaging ever happens on a plain click. Every surface that has things to do to it shares one context menu, and mods add rows to it through providers.

## Principles

- Right-click never destroys.
- Each device, its own habits.
- The pointer can do everything the keyboard does to the camera.
- See an order before it happens, and take it back just after.
